Hello World!

The CSUA supports the undergraduate computer science community at UC Berkeley. Founded in 1972, we're the largest and oldest CS club on campus. We're super chill people who like CS and capybaras.

We host hackathons, socials, industry events and workshops, and give members free computers, servers and tutoring. You do not have to be a CS major to join!
